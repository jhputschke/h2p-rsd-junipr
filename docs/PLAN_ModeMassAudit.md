Four deviations from the text below, each measured rather than assumed:

1. **`certified` is split in two.** `certified` (the whole top-k list is provably exact:
   `pruned < M_k` and the search completed) and `certified_top1` (`pruned < M_1`). The
   plan's single flag conflated a claim about the list with a claim about the mode, and
   only the second is needed for the headline $F(m)$.
2. **A child cap exists but defaults OFF** (`topk_children=0`). It is needed for the
   accounting to stay exact under any memory bound, but on the fielded 30×30 geometry
   capping at 64 took the certified fraction from 97% to 20% for a 28% runtime saving —
   a bad trade, so `prune_rel` does the pruning instead.
3. **An uncertified `M_1` is documented as a LOWER bound**, so every $F(m)$ is a
   one-sided statement rather than an approximation, and $M_1>\tfrac12$ is noted as
   self-certifying whatever the search pruned.
4. **Per-jet record building is factored out** (`eval.mode_audit.audit_jet`) so the
   notebook and the CLI runner produce the same record from the same code — the two
   populations drifting apart is the failure `PLAN_prod_test_v0.md` §7 records.

**Question this answers.** For what fraction of jets does the posterior
$q_\phi(y\mid x)$ concentrate on a *single* discrete configuration with quotable
probability — i.e., does a dominant parton skeleton exist per jet, and when it exists,
is it the true one? This converts the "finite support ⇒ dominant mode" conjecture into
a per-jet measurement with certificates, stratified in the variables that control the
non-perturbative smearing ($\sigma = \sigma_0 + \Lambda_{\rm eff}/k_t$).

---

## 1. The two load-bearing facts

**Fact 1 — the skeleton marginal is exact, no integration required.** Define the
*skeleton* $S = (N,\, c_0 \dots c_{N-1})$: the multiplicity plus the *ordered* cell
sequence (order = primary declustering order, so "split order" is part of $S$). In
`ar_junipr_v2` the per-node likelihood factorizes as
$P_{\rm split}(c_t)\cdot p(\text{coords}\mid c_t)$ where the coordinate factor
(TN·TN·N·vM, truncation normalizers included) is a **proper density given the cell** —
it integrates to 1 over its support (this is exactly what `tests/test_lnz_support.py`'s
MC-normalization test certifies for the physical head). Marginalizing the continuous
coordinates therefore collapses analytically:

$$
q_\phi(S \mid x) \;=\; \Big[\prod_{t=0}^{N-1} P_{\rm cont}(\text{cont}\mid h_t, e)\,
P_{\rm split}(c_t \mid h_t, e)\Big]\; P_{\rm cont}(\text{stop}\mid h_N, e).
$$

Skeleton masses are exact model probabilities, computable from the discrete heads
alone. The empty skeleton ($N{=}0$) is included with mass
$P_{\rm cont}(\text{stop}\mid h_0,e)$ — identical to the $q(0\mid x)$ already studied
in the empty-tree analysis (consistency assert below).

**Fact 2 — prefix mass equals subtree mass, so best-first search is exact.** For a
prefix $c_{0:t}$, the total posterior mass of *all* skeletons extending it equals the
prefix's own accumulated product $\prod_{s<t} P_{\rm cont}\,P_{\rm split}$, because the
remaining factors are normalized and sum to 1 over all futures. A best-first
(uniform-cost) search over the prefix tree with this mass as priority therefore pops
completed skeletons **in exact descending mass order**: when a completed sequence is
popped, every unexplored completion is a descendant of some frontier prefix whose
priority — its exact subtree mass — is not larger. This is Dijkstra on the prefix
tree; the monotone-score best-first framing is Meister, Vieira & Cotterell, *TACL* **8**
(2020) 795, arXiv:2007.03909, and the exact-enumeration-of-NMT-modes precedent is
Stahlberg & Byrne, arXiv:1908.10090. No beam-search approximation is involved:
$M_1 \ge M_2 \ge \dots \ge M_k$ are exact, and dominance statements
($M_1 > \tfrac12$ or $M_1 < \tfrac12$) are **certificates**, not estimates, since the
total mass is 1 by construction.

Bookkeeping invariant (float-exact validity check):

$$
\sum_{i \le k^{\rm found}} M_i \;+\; \underbrace{\textstyle\sum_{\rm heap} m_{\rm prefix}}_{\text{frontier mass}}
\;+\; \underbrace{m_{\rm pruned}}_{\text{tracked exactly}} \;=\; 1 .
$$

Every pruned child's exact mass is added to $m_{\rm pruned}$ at pruning time
($m \cdot p_{\rm cont} \cdot \sum_{c\,\text{dropped}} \mathrm{softmax}[c]$ — closed
form from the same softmax), so coverage $C_k = \sum_{i\le k} M_i$ carries a certified
remainder bound $1 - C_k \le m_{\rm frontier} + m_{\rm pruned}$.

## 2. Key facts (verified against the repo and merged plans)

- The incremental single-step decode API already exists: `_step(tok, e, h) →
  (p_cont, logp_split, h')` — the same path `sample_batch` and
  `map_decode`/`beam_search_cells` (`inference/point_estimate.py:69–103`) consume.
  The search needs nothing new from the model.
- `min_emissions` (PLAN_NsplitMinCut) constrains the *point estimate* only; the audit
  enumerates the **unconstrained** posterior (a floor would distort the mass
  accounting for the same reason a sampling floor would distort SBC/PIT — that
  standing decision carries over verbatim). The empty skeleton is a first-class row.
- Family structure differs but every family's skeleton marginal is exact:
  - `ar_junipr_v1/v2`: the AR product above (identical between v1 and v2 — the audit
    never touches the coordinate heads).
  - `v3` / `v1_nhead` (`use_multiplicity_head`): $q(N\mid x)\cdot\prod_t
    P_{\rm split}(c_t\mid h_t,e;N)$, cells conditioned on the realized $N$ — run a
    fixed-length best-first per $N$ with $q(N\mid x)\ge\varepsilon$ and merge on one
    global heap. Same exactness proof (fixed-length subtree mass = prefix mass).
  - `cinn` / `diffusion`: $q(N\mid e)\cdot\prod_t P(c_t\mid e)$ — cells conditionally
    independent given $e$, so top-k is closed-form lazy k-best over a product of
    sorted categoricals (Huang & Chiang, IWPT 2005 "k-best parsing" idiom); no
    stepping at all.
- `mbr_select` (`inference/mbr.py:345`) already produces per-jet posterior cell chains;
  the audit reuses those draws for the entropy estimate at zero extra sampling cost.
- Parity is trivially untouched: no training-path file changes, no `state_dict`
  change, no `per_jet_nll` change. New config fields follow the tolerant
  `decode_params` / `OmegaConf.select` backfill idiom; `config_hash` moves for new
  runs only, per the standing rule.

## 3. Design decisions

- **Skeleton, not full configuration, is the dominance object.** The continuous
  coordinates never concentrate below the NP width; dominance is only well-posed for
  the discrete structure. When a majority skeleton exists, its kinematics are quoted
  as conditional summaries (per-node conditional mode/mean ± the head's own width,
  κ-gate for ψ respected) via the existing staged machinery — reported as
  "value ± posterior width", never as a bare point.
- **Exact search with certificates, not beam search.** Beam search can miss the true
  top-k; the priority-queue search cannot, and the frontier/pruned accounting turns
  every summary number into a bound. A jet whose search exhausts the expansion budget
  before $k$ completions is flagged `certified: false` and excluded from headline
  fractions (its rate is itself a reported number).
- **Two questions, kept separate in every table:** (i) *does* a dominant skeleton
  exist ($M_1$ distribution, $F(m) = \mathrm{frac}(M_1 \ge m)$); (ii) *is* it correct
  (truth rank/mass on matched pairs). Mode dominance and mode correctness are logically
  independent and the write-up must never conflate them.
- **Entropy is skeleton-marginal entropy.** $\hat H = -\frac1K\sum_k \log
  q_\phi(S^{(k)}\mid x)$ over posterior draws is an unbiased estimator of
  $H(S\mid x)$; $e^{\hat H}$ is the effective skeleton count (typical-set framing:
  Cover & Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, ch. 3). The enumerated-set entropy
  $-\sum_i M_i \log M_i$ is reported as its certified lower bound.
- **Descriptive audit, not a gated intervention.** Nothing is adopted or rejected on
  these numbers, so there are no pass/fail gates — but the *quoted quantities and
  strata are pre-registered here* (§7) before the run, per the standing principle.
  Two validity checks (not physics gates) do apply: the mass-accounting invariant and
  the sampled-frequency consistency check (§8, T4).
- **Device note.** The search is inherently sequential per jet; run on CPU (per-step
  MPS sync overhead dominates at dec_dim 64). An optional micro-batched variant
  (pop $m$ nodes, batch the GRU steps) is deferred to the same WP that fixes the
  71× batched-sampling penalty — do not couple the audit to it.

## 4. WP-1 — enumeration core (`inference/mode_audit.py`)

```python
@dataclass
class SkeletonEnumeration:
    skeletons: list[tuple[list[int], float]]  # (cells, log_mass), descending, exact
    frontier_log_mass: float                  # logsumexp over remaining heap
    pruned_log_mass: float                    # exact, accumulated at prune time
    certified: bool                           # k completions before budget
    n_expansions: int

def enumerate_skeletons(model, xf, nx, *, k=64, budget=20_000,
                        prune_rel=1e-6, max_emissions=25) -> SkeletonEnumeration
```

Algorithm (AR families): heap of `(−log_mass, cells, h)` seeded with the empty prefix
at $h_0 = \tanh(W e)$; pop → `_step` → push the stop-completion at
$\ell + \log(1-p_{\rm cont})$ and each surviving cell child at
$\ell + \log p_{\rm cont} + \log P_{\rm split}[c]$; children below
`prune_rel × current_kth_mass` (or below an absolute floor before $k$ completions)
go to `pruned_log_mass` in closed form. Frontier capped (mass-ordered eviction into
`pruned_log_mass`) to bound memory. Terminate at $k$ completions or `budget`
expansions. `max_emissions` carried from the existing decode contract.

```python
def skeleton_log_prob(model, cells, xf, nx) -> float
```

Teacher-forced accumulation of the discrete factors along one given chain (O(N)
`_step` calls). Used for (a) the truth skeleton's exact mass, (b) scoring the reused
MBR posterior draws for $\hat H$.

## 5. WP-2 — family adapters

- Base: `PosteriorModel.skeleton_search_spec()` returning a small strategy object
  (`ar` | `nhead` | `factorized`); default raises `NotImplementedError` with the
  family name (loud, per house style).
- `ar_junipr_v1/v2`: WP-1 verbatim.
- `n_head` family: per-$N$ fixed-length search over $\{N : q(N\mid x) \ge
  \varepsilon_N\}$ (pre-register $\varepsilon_N = 10^{-4}$; its total dropped mass
  goes to `pruned_log_mass`), merged on one heap. Note this family is where the G8
  winner lives, so this adapter is **not** optional.
- `cinn`/`diffusion`: closed-form lazy k-best from the categorical heads; exact by
  construction, `certified` always true.

  read via the `decode_params`-style helper (no direct `cfg.audit.<field>` reads).
- Artifact `mode_audit.json`: per-jet records
  `{M: [...], cells_top1, M1, M2, log_M1_M2, C_k, frontier, pruned, certified,
  H_hat, n_x, lnkt_lead, d_boundary, d_floor, M_truth, rank_truth, top1_is_truth}`
  plus a run-level summary block containing exactly the §7 quantities. Backend and
  `fitted_under`-style provenance fields as usual.
- Stratification: reuse the existing `stratify_regions` quadrants, plus three new
  per-jet scalars computed from $x$: leading-node $\ln k_t$; boundary distance
  $d_B = \min_t (\ln z_t - \ln z_{\rm cut})$ (for $\beta = 0$ the SD boundary is the
  straight line $z = z_{\rm cut}$ in this basis); floor distance
  $d_F = \min_t(\ln k_t^{(t)} - \ln k_t^{\rm floor})$. These are the axes on which the
  $\sigma_0 + \Lambda_{\rm eff}/k_t$ scaling predicts the dominant/fragmented mixture
  to separate.
- Notebook: one new `inference_demo.ipynb` section — $F(m)$ curves overall and per
  stratum, $M_1$ vs $\ln k_t^{\rm lead}$ scatter, truth-rank table — plus the
  consistency asserts (§8 T5/T6 patterns).

## 7. Pre-registered report quantities (fixed before the run)

1. $P_{50}$, $P_{90}$ of $M_1$; $F(0.3)$, $F(0.5)$, $F(0.7)$ with 95% Wilson
   intervals (Brown, Cai & DasGupta, *Statist. Sci.* **16** (2001) 101), overall and
   per stratum; `certified` fraction alongside every number.
2. $\mathrm{frac}(S_{\rm truth} = S_{\rm top1})$, $\mathrm{frac}(S_{\rm truth} \in$
   top-3, top-10$)$, median $M_{\rm truth}$ — same strata. (Direct answer to the
   conjecture's second half.)
3. $\hat H$ distribution and $e^{\hat H}$; Spearman correlations of $M_1$ with
   $\ln k_t^{\rm lead}$, $d_B$, $d_F$, and $N_x$ (predicted signs: +, +, +, −).
4. The mixture-population statement, quoted as: $F(0.5)$ in the
   (high-$k_t$ ∧ $d_B, d_F$ large) stratum vs. $F(0.5)$ in its complement.
5. Cross-family delta on the shared checkpoint pair: `v1_base` vs `v1_nhead`
   (does the explicit-$N$ factorization change dominance, or only calibration?).

## 8. Tests (`tests/test_mode_audit.py`)

- **T1 — brute-force exactness.** Tiny random model (`n_cells ≤ 4`,
  `max_emissions ≤ 4`): exhaustive enumeration of all skeletons by direct product;
  best-first output must match masses *and order* to 1e-6, and total mass = 1.
- **T2 — certificate property.** Random budgets/prune settings: popped completions
  are monotone non-increasing; completed + frontier + pruned = 1 within tolerance at
  every termination point.
- **T3 — scorer consistency.** `skeleton_log_prob` of a teacher-forced chain equals
  the discrete-head part of the likelihood accumulated via `_step` (and, on a chain
  sampled by `sample_batch` with fixed seed, the two paths agree bitwise-ish, atol
  1e-5 per the parity convention).
- **T4 — sampled-frequency consistency.** Fixed seed, $10^4$ draws: empirical
  top-1 frequency inside the exact binomial 99% interval of $M_1$.
- **T5 — empty-skeleton identity.** Enumerated $M_{N=0}$ equals the model's
  $q(0\mid x)$ (the `empty_threshold_for_rate` bookkeeping quantity) to float
  tolerance.
- **T6 — n_head adapter.** Per-$N$ merge vs brute force on a tiny `n_head` model.
- Run `scripts/verify_parity.py` + `tests/test_parity.py` as always — expected to be
  vacuous (no training-path diff), asserted anyway.

## 10. Compute

Worst case `budget` = 2·10⁴ `_step` calls/jet at ~O(10⁴) FLOP each — seconds/jet on
CPU, and typical jets terminate orders of magnitude earlier (mass concentration is
the phenomenon under study). The 2 000-jet closure tier is a single evening run;
retroactive passes on the v0 and v1 checkpoints included.

## 11. Non-goals (deferred, with triggers)

- **Batched multi-jet best-first** — trigger: audit throughput binds at the
  higher-statistics closure tier; couples to the 71× sampling-speed WP, not here.
- **Importance-sampled mass estimates** — unnecessary; the exact search exists.
- **Coordinate-level mode quoting beyond the staged/κ-gate machinery** — the NP width
  is irreducible; no new coordinate point-estimate semantics.
- **Any decode-layer behavior change** (MBR, MAP, floors) — the audit reads the
  posterior; it never writes to the estimator stack.
